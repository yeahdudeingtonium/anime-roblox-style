# Idea Bank (2026-11-14)

**154 ideas**, each built as a proven parent plus one change (types from [[Mutation Playbook]]), and **every title checked against live Roblox search**. It's for brainstorming: pick a lane, then run the full checks in [[Idea Judgement Rules]] before committing.

Raw results: `Data/idea-bank-check-2026-11-14.json` (round 1, 117 ideas) and `Data/idea-bank-check-r2-2026-11-14.json` (round 2, 37 ideas). CCU is live from a Saturday snapshot.

## TL;DR
- **The hottest new wave is ragdoll physics:** Hyper Realistic Ragdoll 145K (15 days), Bloody Ragdoll 35K, Ragdoll Soccer 27K, Ragdoll Physics 24K, Ragdoll Research 19K, BloxNG 15K. 🔍 The leaders are **single-player sandboxes with no progression**. Adding a goal (sport, progression, inverted role) is the open gap.
- **Noun-swap graveyards, confirmed again:**
  - Throw Food For Animals reskins sit at 0–161 CCU (Candy for Monsters 1, Food For Dinos 18, Food For Anime 35).
  - "Steal a ___" reskins sit at 0–20 (Ghost 0, Treasure 0, Dragon Egg 5, Superpower copy 3).
  - The bug-wave copies sit at 2–55.
  - The "Can You Pass A ___?" copies sit at 11–143.
- **Single-verb wildcards are mostly dead:** Shake a Tree 10, Pop a Bubble 0, Grow a Giant 1, Inflate a Balloon 3, Wake the Dragon 0.

## Patterns from these 154 checks
1. **About 75 of the 154 titles are already taken, and about 90% of those are under 1K.** Nearly every obvious reskin has been tried and failed within the last 60 days.
2. **The open lanes cluster around the newest parents** (ragdoll wave, Stop The Tsunami, Sweep The Moon, Jump for Cases), because cloners haven't caught up yet. That's the case for moving fast on a young parent (rules 2 and 18).
3. **The gap in a new wave is usually what the leader lacks:** a goal, progression or multiplayer. The ragdoll leaders are solo sandboxes, and Ragdoll Soccer (structure + 8 players) is the proof that filling it works.
4. **Seasonal lanes are empty by title but historically weak.** Winter needs to sit on a proven loop, not be the hook.
