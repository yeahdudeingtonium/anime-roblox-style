# Idea Ledger Round 2: Young Waves

A quick round aimed at the **youngest waves on the charts** (originator under about 30 days old), judged with [[Idea Judgement Rules]]. All data comes from live `tools/roblox_intel.py search` on 2026-10-31 (Halloween Saturday snapshot).

## TL;DR
- **Best timing bet: "[Verb] to Escape".** The originator, Punch to Escape, is 14 days old with 7K CCU. **Kick to Escape** is open (the only near match, Kick Door To Escape, is 336 days old with 303 CCU). It needs a ≤3-week build to land inside the clone window.
- **Clean the Moon** (6.6K at 19 days) suggests an absurd-chore-at-giant-scale wave. A verb swap such as **Polish the Moon** or **Mine the Moon** is a fresh lane.
- **The anime pick is +1 Anime Hair**, riding +1 Anime Eyes (32K at 16 days). The risk is that it reads as a noun swap.
- **This round confirmed rules 4 and 6 hard:** the noun-swap and chained-title copies launched in the last 2 weeks are all near zero.

## Lessons (feed into [[Idea Judgement Rules]])
- **Noun-swap copies launched in the last 2 weeks are near zero:** Fake a Pet 12, Sell Popcorn 186. That strongly confirms rule 6.
- **Copying chained titles fails immediately:** Ride and Steal a Pet sits at 12–15. That confirms rule 4.
- **A copy's like % drops when it's a noun swap** (Sell Popcorn 45%, Fake a Pet 69%), which feeds back into Recommended for You.
- **"+1 [body part]" is a lottery:** Anime Eyes 32K, Tongue 5.4K, Tail 3. The body part has to be a fantasy players want to *be*.
