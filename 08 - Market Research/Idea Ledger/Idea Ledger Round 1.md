# Idea Ledger, Round 1

Every idea got one or two live Roblox searches on 2026-10-31, roughly 210 in total (cached raw results sit in the session scratchpad and are not committed). CCU means live concurrent players at search time and `d` means age in days. Treat each number as a single snapshot, not an average. Halloween is boosting some games today.

**Verdicts.** **Open** means nothing relevant exists, or only old games under 100 CCU. **Beatable** means a similar game is at 1K or more and there's room for a twist. **Graveyard** means similar games exist and all sit under 1K. **Owned** means one game at 10K or more dominates.

Pattern-mining baseline (coordinator, 1,336 live games aged 120 days or less): 23% reach 1K and 4% reach 10K. "[Verb] For Eggs/Animals" reaches 1K 45% of the time and "[Verb] to Steal" 45%. Steal-noun swaps hit 33% with no 10K game. "+1" overall is 19%, "Merge" is 0 of 24, and anime skins sit at the 24% baseline.

### Lessons learned, batch 1
- **The theme alone never carried a game.** Kaiju, KPop demons, bubbles and dragons died even with good verbs. The ≥1K games pair a *concrete physical verb* with a *reward kids already want* (egg, pet, animal, food).
- **Noun-swapping a hit is the worst move.** Ride a Dino has 22K, but its literal copy has 525, Ride a Fish 334 and Ride a Dragon 925.
- **The anime and aura lane is real but capped at 2–7K.** Anime Dice and +1 Anime Eyes won with a format-first title. Anime is a skin.
- **The verbs that work today:** throw, fake, wash, chop, ride (first mover only), feed and steal. **Dead:** shake, pop, bonk, summon, hatch, plus kaiju and demon-idol as nouns.
- Steer for batch 2: mutate the *verb* and *wrapper* of young ≥1K games (Feed a Mosquito, Wash the Car, Sell Fries, Bike For Egg, Cook the Chicken, Harpoon a Fish, Roll A Stickman). Test winter themes for the Dec 12–15 launch.

### Lessons learned, batch 2
- **Keep the reward and swap the verb (works). Keep the verb and swap the noun (flops).** Bike For Egg 46K, Climb For Animals Eggs 45K and Hike For Eggs 14K are different verbs chasing the same egg. Ride a Fish, Wash The Plane and Evolve A Dragon are the same verb on a new noun, and they flopped.
- **Food is the hottest noun family:** Sell Fries 47K, Cook the Chicken 16K, Eat Everything In A Grocery Store 8.7K at 27 days, Make Ramen 3.5K at 22 days, Ice Cream Shop 3.4K at 17 days.
- **Downhill and slide works:** Drive a Cart Downhill 9.7K at 27 days and SLED SURFERS 3.3K at 18 days. That makes a sled winter twist plausible.
- **Winter and snow are empty right now** (everything under 500 CCU). That is an open lane for a Dec 12–15 launch, with no proof yet.
- **Dragon, dino and kaiju are dead nouns** unless the game is first (coordinator: 14% hit 1K).
- Steer for batch 3: "[new verb] For Eggs/Animals" with winter verbs, food stands with a winter skin, and "+1 X Leveling".

### Lessons learned, batch 3
- **The For-Eggs template has a decay curve.** First movers got 45K, mid copies 3–4K (Swim, Jump, Swing) and late copies about 900 (Surf, Wings, Climb and Slide). Even late copies sit near 1K, so the floor is high, but you only get a hit with an **unclaimed, visual verb**. Sled is still unclaimed.
- **Waves move fast.** Punch to Escape is 14 days old and already has three copies at 1–3.6K. By December this wave will be saturated. Chase verbs whose wave hasn't started.
- **Food eating and selling keeps producing:** three Eat-the-Supermarket games all ≥1K inside 27 days.
- **"Evolution" only works with a fresh body or creature fantasy** (Venom, Bug). Fish, Hero and Saiyan versions are stuck at about 1K or below, which the coordinator's +1 data confirms.
- Steer for batch 4: Christmas mutations of proven verbs (dig, climb, open all, steal, eat, paint), a final anime pass, and "[Verb] to Escape".

### Lessons learned, batch 5 (and overall)
1. **What predicts ≥1K:** a 2–4 word title with a visible verb, plus egg/pet/animal/food as the reward, plus first-mover status on that verb. Coordinator: the 10K rate is 7% for 2-word titles and 2% for 4+.
2. **Mutate the verb, keep the reward.** Bike, Climb and Hike For Eggs all won with different verbs on the same egg. Noun swaps flop (Ride a Fish 334, Wash The Plane 159, Evolve A Dragon 427). Copies of chained-verb titles have a median of 70 CCU (coordinator).
3. **Deception and steal verbs** (Fake an Egg 43K, Hide to Steal Eggs 10K, Fly Tesla Roadster To Steal Eggs 7K at 12 days) are the strongest young grammar after For-Eggs.
4. **Dead:** hatch, shake, pop, bonk, summon, lasso, shovel, merge. Kaiju, dragon, demon idol, penguin, bubble and slime as nouns. Plain "+1".
5. **Winter is a blank map today.** It's a timing bet for Dec 12–15, so pair it with a proven loop and ship 15–60 days before the peak (median 10K game is 40 days old).
6. **Discovery:** new games first reach 16+ players, so the idea must not look babyish. Steal, fake, escape and sled-physics games pass. Squishy and wash games are weaker.
