# Anime Core Ideas (2026-11-14)

The owner's filter: **anime as the core fantasy**, on simple, easy-to-monetize ("cash grab") loops.

Excluded categories: steal, For Eggs, lucky, tycoon, grow, clicker, pets, ragdoll/physics, party, space chores, obby, anomaly jobs, anime TD, X Days.

Two batches were checked live against Roblox search: **100 front-page-inspired ideas** (`Data/idea-bank-check-r6-2026-11-14.json`) and **100 anime-core ideas** (`Data/idea-bank-check-r7-anime-2026-11-14.json`). Titles are open *by title*; rule 19 (check the mechanics inside the top games) was applied to the biggest anime-adjacent hits only.

## The rule this adds (proposed rule 20)
**In the +1 / Forge cash-grab formats, the fantasy must be a specific, iconic power or weapon players already want (Sukuna, Power Fruit, Ghoul), not a generic category (Cursed, Aura, Sword, Anime).** Generic-category versions sit at 0–300 CCU; iconic ones reach 2–21K.
