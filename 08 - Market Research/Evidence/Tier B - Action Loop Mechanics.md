# Tier B: Action Loop Mechanics ("the title is the verb")

**Scope:** simple "do one action" Roblox games, 2024 to 2026, with a focus on 2025 to 2026 breakouts. Audience lens: solo dev, R6 blocky style, kids 8 to 14, ships in 4 to 6 weeks.
**Method:** 37 WebSearch queries covering 25 games. WebFetch was **blocked** in this session for allthings.how, beebom.com, rotrends.com and rowatcher.com (DNS refused). **Nearly all facts below come from search-result snippets**, not full-page reads. CCU numbers come from third-party trackers (RoWatcher, RoTrends) or press (PocketGamer.biz, Insider Gaming, Wikipedia).
**Stale rule:** evidence older than 60 days (before 2026-09-01) is tagged **[STALE]**. Most CCU peaks are from 2025, so they are stale by definition. They remain the record peaks, but they do not describe current health.

Loop notation: `verb -> reward -> spend -> stronger verb -> rebirth`

## 2. Top 10 dissected

**1. Grow a Garden (2025-03-26, peak 21.3M on 2025-06-21) [STALE CCU].** The verb, plant, is slow and asynchronous. The game is fun because of what happens between sessions: crops grow offline, so every login opens on a payoff. Rarity comes from seed restock timers (FOMO) and weather/admin mutations (gamble). The garden is a public showcase on a shared server. Rebirth is barely there. Depth comes instead from pets and the Pet Mutation Machine (age-50 pet, 500M Sheckles, 60 min). Reportedly built by a teen in about 3 days (PC Gamer). Lesson: **offline growth + public plot + timed restock + mutation RNG** beats depth. Sources: [PocketGamer.biz 21.3M](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-grow-a-garden-hits-industry-record-213m-concurrent-users-surpassing-pubg-fortnite-and-more), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden), [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/a-roblox-farming-game-made-by-a-teenager-in-like-three-days-had-8-9-million-players-online-at-the-same-time-steam-peaked-at-11-5-million-across-all-games-on-the-same-day), [esports.gg pet mutations](https://esports.gg/guides/roblox/grow-a-garden-a-complete-pet-mutations-guide/), [GamePressure offline pets](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/pets-dont-level-up-and-grow-offline-in-grow-a-garden-gag-anymore/z483c6).

**2. Steal a Brainrot (May 2025, peak 25.4M+ Oct 2025) [STALE CCU].** Buy a unit off a conveyor and it prints $/sec on your plot. Anyone can walk in and carry it away. The base-lock timer (30-60 s, +10 s per rebirth) creates timed raid windows. Rebirth wipes brainrots and cash, so you're incentivized to "protect" units on alts or fuse them. Lesson: **stealing turns an idle tycoon into a social, adrenaline loop**. This is the most cloned skeleton of 2025-26 (Steal an Anime, Break a Lucky Block, Build a Zoo, Escape Tsunami). Sources: [PocketGamer.biz 25M](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot), [Beebom rebirth](https://beebom.com/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-guide-levels-and-rewards/), [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/steal-a-brainrot-roblox-efficient-stealing-events-and-rebirths).

**3. My Anime Mine (2026-08-19, 745 peak ~2026-09-11) [inside window].** Developer is Curseddd Games. You start in The Underground and mine rock. Mixed in with the ores are **anime characters** (Naruto- and Frieren-style). Once you dig one up, you **add it to your crew, and the crew mines and earns money for you passively**. That is the auto-verb, and it is the game's whole pitch. Zones are bought in order from a signboard. Crystal Caves, the second zone, costs $100,000. Each zone has its own **research tree** (e.g. Luck I, Deposits I, Density I) that raises ore richness and rare-character odds. **Enchanted Dice** roll or upgrade characters, **Mutation Luck** boosts rare mutations (stronger stats and mining power), and **Summon Scrolls** grant pulls. The intro tutorial gates the codes menu. No source documents a rebirth or offline earnings (one Roblox page says "your heroes will earn cash OFFLINE", but that page could not be tied to this game). Loop: `mine -> cash + anime char -> crew mines for you -> research/zones -> rarer chars + mutations -> (rebirth unknown)`. CCU is small, so treat it as a **design reference, not a proven hit**. Sources: [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/my-anime-mine-codes), [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/my-anime-mine-codes/), [allthings.how guide](https://allthings.how/?p=266398) (snippet only), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/my-anime-mine-codes-3413496/), [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/my-anime-mine-codes/), [RoTrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10740316645) (snippet only), [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/my-anime-mine-codes-september-2026/).

**4. Fish It! (2024-10-21, peak 1.4M-2.7M) [STALE].** Fish It! is a "luck-based simplicity" rebuttal to skill-based Fisch. You cast, roll a fish from a "1 in X" table with mutations, sell it, and upgrade your rod (Luck/Speed/Weight) and enchants. **Auto-fishing** is built in. Updates ship every Sunday. Growth reportedly came through Roblox's recommendation algorithm rather than influencers. Lesson: **the RNG reveal is the verb's payoff, and auto-mode removes friction for kids**. Sources: [roonby](https://roonby.com/2025/11/11/why-fish-it-became-one-of-robloxs-most-popular-games-but-nobody-talks-about-it/amp/), [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/6701277882/upd-fish-it/dying), [Destructoid enchants](https://www.destructoid.com/complete-roblox-fish-it-rod-enchant-tier-list-guide/), [Beebom enchants](https://beebom.com/roblox-fish-it-enchantment-guide).

**5. Plants vs Brainrots (Aug 2025, peak 3.2M late Sep 2025) [STALE].** Plants auto-defend lanes against brainrots, and you catch the brainrots for income. The tower-defense "auto-verb" makes the game watchable while idle. Sources: [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/robloxs-newest-phenomenon-plants-vs-brainrots-breaks-records-heres-why-everyones-obsessed/), [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/mmo/with-21-million-concurrent-players-roblox-hit-grow-a-garden-is-bigger-than-half-of-steam-combined-but-players-are-already-flooding-another-far-weirder-competitor).

**6. Catch and Tame (2025-11-02, peak 222.1K) [STALE].** Lasso a wandering pet, put it in a pen, and it earns. This is the GaG/SaB idle plot with a physical catch verb and no stealing. Sources: [RoTrends](https://rotrends.com/game/9091133975), [PCGamesN codes](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/catch-and-tame-codes), [earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/catch-and-tame-vs-grow-a-garden).

**7. Escape Tsunami for Brainrots (late 2025, >200K early 2026) [STALE].** Sprint out, grab a brainrot, and return before the wave. Anything you carry when the wave hits is lost. **The hazard timer stands in for stealing as the risk.** Admin-abuse events bring exclusive brainrots. Sister template: Survive Lava for Brainrots (12 rebirths, up to 7x cash, 44 R$ rebirth skip). Sources: [designer-daily](https://www.designer-daily.com/how-to-play-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-a-beginners-guide-211326), [allthings.how money](https://allthings.how/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-money-guide-fastest-ways-to-scale-your-income), [Beebom lava rebirth](https://beebom.com/survive-lava-for-brainrots-rebirth-guide).

**8. Climb and Jump Tower (2025-04-02, peak 110-114K Jun 2025) [STALE].** You climb a tower and earn Wins at the top. Worlds cost exponentially more Wins (80K -> 400K -> 50M -> 1B). There's no rebirth. The world ladder *is* the prestige. Sources: [Deltia Dubai](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=249546), [Sportskeeda World 14](https://widgets.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/climb-jump-tower-world-14-guide), [RoTrends](https://rotrends.com/game/7474367816/Climb-and-Jump-Tower-NEW-WORLD).

**9. +1 Speed family (2024-2026; Monkey Escape 1.5M Aug 2026 per one RoWatcher page, 165K per another) [STALE, conflicting].** The stat rises on its own every second, so number-go-up is literal. Rebirth multipliers come at roughly level 15-25. This is the cheapest template on Roblox, and it's re-skinned endlessly (Keyboard, Monkey, Baby, Slime, Lucky Block). Sources: [RoWatcher Monkey Escape](https://rowatcher.com/games/10144280947/1-speed-monkey-escape), [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/?p=3271), [Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/16243676208).

**10. Spin a Baddie (2025-12-07/08, ~8.5K avg, low confidence).** Spin dice for an anime-styled "baddie" and place it on your plot for income. Each rebirth adds +10% income, and rebirth tiers unlock trading of rarer tiers (R14/R16/R18). Codes require joining the group, which grows the group. Sources: [Pocket Tactics rebirth](https://www.pockettactics.com/spin-a-baddie-rebirth), [PCGamesN codes](https://www.pcgamesn.com/spin-a-baddie/codes), [RoTrends](https://rotrends.com/game/9319914497), [fan FAQ](https://spinabaddie-info.pages.dev/faq).

Honorable mentions: **Break a Lucky Block** (break -> brainrot -> run past chaser NPC "Sammy"; [techwiser](https://techwiser.com/break-a-lucky-block-beginner-guide/), [RoWatcher surge](https://rowatcher.com/news/break-a-lucky-block-surges-to-12k-players-4-4-its-baseline)); **Dig to Earth's CORE!** (vertical dig + pets; [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/dig-earth-s-core-a-beginner-s-guide), [RoTrends](https://rotrends.com/game/7468338447)); **Build a Zoo** ([Deltia](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=348789)); **My Brainrot Miner** (pets mine for you, the closest analogue to My Anime Mine; [Deltia](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=349243)).

## 3. Pattern template: what the breakouts share

1. **Verb-first, 3-4 word title, with a noun the kids already meme about:** "Grow a Garden", "Steal a Brainrot", "Catch and Tame", "Spin a Baddie". The title is the tutorial.
2. **One input, one animation.** Cast, swing, tap, carry. You can play it within 10 seconds on mobile.
3. **The unit is the money printer.** What you collect (brainrot, plant, pet, anime char, baddie) goes on **your plot and earns $/sec**. This plot-income skeleton is shared by SaB, GaG, Catch and Tame, Build a Zoo, Spin a Baddie, Break a Lucky Block, Steal an Anime and My Anime Mine.
4. **Auto-verb / offline payoff.** Crops grow offline (GaG), auto-fishing (Fish It), the crew mines for you (My Anime Mine), pets mine (My Brainrot Miner), the stat ticks +1/sec (+1 Speed). Logging in always pays.
5. **Rarity gamble on every action.** "1 in X" fish, mutations, dice, lucky blocks, conveyor rarity. Secret/Godly tiers get admin-abuse/event exclusives.
6. **One risk or social lever.** Stealing (SaB), a hazard wave/lava (Tsunami/Lava), a chaser NPC (Lucky Block), or at least a public showcase plot (GaG, Zoo). Breakouts without a risk lever peak about 10x lower.
7. **Rebirth = wipe units + permanent multiplier (+10% to 7x),** often gating something social (trading tiers, base-lock seconds, extra slots). A world ladder works as an alternative (Climb and Jump, Dig to Earth's Core).
8. **Monetization sits on the gamble and the clock:** luck potions, dice, restock skips, rebirth skips (44 R$), extra plot slots, base locks. Group-join gating on codes.
9. **Weekly live-ops cadence** (Fish It's Sunday updates; admin-abuse events in SaB/Tsunami/GaG).

**Template line:** `[VERB] a [MEME-NOUN] -> unit rolls rarity -> place on plot for $/sec (works offline) -> buy better tool/zone -> risk lever (steal / hazard / chaser) -> rebirth for multiplier + social unlock`.

## 4. Caveats

- **WebFetch blocked** for allthings.how, beebom.com, rotrends.com and rowatcher.com. All facts from these domains are **search-snippet summaries**. Lift the network restriction to verify them.
- **CCU conflicts are common:** Fish It (1.4M vs 2.7M), Dig to Earth's Core (60.1K vs 4K), +1 Speed Monkey Escape (1.5M vs 165K), Build a Zoo (95.2K vs ~17K implied). Trackers sample differently, and some pages may mix up place IDs.
- **Most peaks are 2025 = [STALE]** relative to 2026-10-31. Current CCU for the brainrot wave has fallen sharply (SaB ~215K in a RoWatcher snapshot, undated).
- **My Anime Mine:** rebirth and offline income are **not confirmed**. Its CCU (745 peak) makes it a mechanic reference rather than a market proof.
- **Ambiguous titles:** "Lift Legends", "Bake a Cake", "Dig It", "Mine for Brainrots" and "+1 Speed Every Second" each map to several games. I picked the most-covered match and flagged it in each row.
- Titles in the brief I could not substantiate as 2025-26 hits: Lift Legends (17-player peak), Bake a Cake (no CCU), Dig It (no reliable CCU).
- Revenue figures (GaG $12M/month, $285M lifetime) are third-party estimates.
