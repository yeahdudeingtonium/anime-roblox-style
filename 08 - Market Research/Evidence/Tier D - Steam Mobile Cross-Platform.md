# Tier D: Steam, Mobile and Cross-Platform Loop Scan

**Question:** Which simple core loops are hot on Steam, mobile and web portals in 2025–2026 but are *not yet saturated* on Roblox, and could become a one-action R6 blocky game for kids aged 8–14, shippable in 4–6 weeks?

**Method:** 61 WebSearch queries run on 2026-10-31. WebFetch was blocked for every host tried: `mobilegamer.biz` and `hitmarker.net` both returned ENOTFOUND, which means they are outside this session's allowed network domains. **All figures below come from WebSearch result snippets**, not from pages opened directly. Roblox CCU figures are third-party tracker snapshots (RoWatcher, RoTrends, Bloxodes) taken from those snippets. I did not query Roblox's own search, so the clone counts are lower bounds.

**Legend**
- **Stale** = the latest evidence is more than 60 days old, i.e. dated before 2026-09-01.
- **Clones >1K** = the number of Roblox clones I found with an all-time peak above 1K CCU. "0 found" means no such clone turned up in searches; it does not prove none exist.
- **Fit** = how well the loop ports to R6 blocky style for kids 8–14 in 4–6 weeks, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).

## 3. Rejected for audience or policy reasons

- **Gamble With Your Friends** (42.8K peak, May 2026; 1–2M sold). A casino loop for an 8–14 audience runs into Roblox's gambling restrictions. Rejected.
- **WARDOGS** (428.7K peak, Sep 13 2026; 3M sold). A military shooter; it doesn't fit the brief.
- **Mage Arena** (13.4K, Jul 2025, stale). Voice-cast spells, but Roblox voice chat is age-gated (13+), which rules out most of the target audience.
- **Slay the Spire 2** (574.6K peak, Mar 2026). Deckbuilding is too deep for a one-action game.

## 4. Notes

- **The Roblox clone window is about 2–3 weeks.** Meccha Chameleon (released Jun 9–10) had clones beating it within 3 weeks, and Clean the Library launched about a month after Librarian peaked. Viral Steam co-op games get cloned almost immediately. For a 4–6 week build, the realistic play is to (a) target a hit that is *days* old and simple (Worst Class Ever: Next Fest #1, full release Nov 9 2026), or (b) target a *mobile/PC single-player* loop that Roblox cloners tend to ignore (Pixel Flow, Ball x Pit, Bus jam, Arrows).
- **Steam's October 2026 pipeline:** Next Fest Oct 19–26 shows the trends are social deduction (Worst Class Ever), shop sims (Probably Stolen), and "haystack" incremental games (seven incremental games in the top 50).
- **The mobile hybrid-casual hits of 2026 are spatial sorting puzzles** (Arrows, Bus Fever, Magic Sort, Pixel Flow, Block Blast). Their Roblox ports so far are weak (Blast a Block peaked at 318), which suggests the puzzle loop needs a multiplayer/physical twist to work on Roblox. Pixel Flow's shooter verb is the most "Roblox-native" of the group.
- **"Friendslop" is still growing** (Game World Observer, Aug 2026: "many games... but not oversaturated"). Sensor Tower counted 33 indie breakouts with 500K+ units in 30 days (Sep 2025–Aug 2026), 9 of them friendslop.
- **Web portals:** In Oct 2026, Poki's top games are evergreen (Subway Surfers, Block Blast, Scary Teacher 3D, Level Devil 2, Little Farm World, Stickman Party 2), so they offer no fresh loops. I found no usable CrazyGames trending data (the search returned sitemap pages only).
- **UEFN:** The flow runs Roblox to Fortnite (Steal the Brainrot); I found no UEFN-originated loop worth porting.

## 5. Caveats

- WebFetch was blocked, so every number comes from a search snippet. Trackers (SteamDB, steamcharts, Raijin, RoWatcher) disagree and the snapshots are undated in places; ranges are reported where they disagree.
- How to Fish's peak is 268,101 in most sources, but one secondary source (refurbo.in) says 370,901. Use 268K–371K.
- Worst Class Ever's demo figures (300K vs 600K players) come from the developer; Valve's list ranks by weekly unique players.
- Roblox clone counts are lower bounds from search, not from a Roblox catalog crawl. **Before committing, verify on Roblox search/RoWatcher**: "teacher prank hide", "bonk fish", "golf party", "pixel shooter", "pawn shop", "ball pit roguelike".
- Stale rows (Super Battle Golf, Ball x Pit, Megabonk, Mewgenics, Oaken Tower, Arrows) show *proven* loops, not current momentum.
- No CrazyGames trending list or fortnite.gg October leaderboard could be retrieved.
