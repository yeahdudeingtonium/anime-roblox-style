# Tier A: Roblox Velocity Evidence Log

Scope: simple "do one action" core loops that are **rising** (last 14–30 days) as of 2026-10-31.
Method: about 45 WebSearch queries. **rowatcher.com could not be fetched from this session (DNS blocked)**, and other trackers were not fetched either, so every figure below comes from **search-result snippets** of RoWatcher, RoTrends, profitable.app and Bloxodes pages, plus code/guide sites. Labels: **TRENDING** = rising in the last 14–30 days. **PROVEN** = big but flat or declining. **STALE** = newest evidence older than 60 days (before about 2026-09-01). **WATCH** = weak or one-source signal.

## Title word fads
1. **"Steal ___ (an) Egg"**: the dominant wave of Aug–Oct 2026. Steal An Egg is #1 on the platform, Break and Steal an Egg hit about 1.3M, Steal Animal Egg hit 107K, Steal Fish Eggs grew +447%, and there are dozens of skins (Dino, Fish, Giant, Hello Kitty, Lucky, Claw, Jump To, Grow Beanstalk To, Clean and…). RoWatcher's own headline says "Egg Games… Rule the Week". This is the 2026 successor to "Steal a Brainrot", and the clones are saturating it (most peak below 1K).
2. **Egg -> hatch -> pen/plot income**: the shared mechanic under Steal-Egg, Ride A Pet and Steal Fish Eggs. The "egg" noun is the wave, not just "Steal".
3. **"Ride a ___"**: Ride A Pet went from +1,753% to a 1.6M peak. Older variants (Ride a Squishy Dumpling, Ride A Friend Battlegrounds) exist.
4. **"Anime ___"** with a gacha verb (Dice / Roll / Mine / Capture / Egg Farm): Anime Dice is the clear riser (up to 93K). "My Anime Mine" shows that the anime + mine combo alone did not take off (745 peak).
5. **"Fish / Fisch"**: still recurring (How to Fisch! +426%, Steal Fish Eggs, Fish It proven).
6. **Imperative-negative 2-word horror titles**: "DONT SPLASH" (about 79K in 8 days) and "7 Days Cat-Sitting" (+825%). This is the Halloween season horror lift (W40 hottest genre was Survival +45%).
7. **Fading:** "+1 ___ Every Second" (newest code pages dated about Aug 1, so STALE), "Escape Tsunami For Brainrots" (conflicting, stale), "Brainrot" in general (RoWatcher ran an "Is the Brainrot Trend Dying?" piece), "Grow a ___" (GAG2 is big but flat), "Dig/Mine" (no new Oct breakout found), "Sell ___" (Sell Lemons declining).

## Data caveats
- **Primary sources were not fetched.** WebFetch to rowatcher.com failed with DNS ENOTFOUND (blocked in this session). Every number is from search snippets, whose snapshot time is often missing. rowatcher.com needs to be allowed in network settings to verify.
- **The October weekly digests (W41–W43) were not found or indexed.** The newest digest available is W40 (ending Oct 4), so velocity for Oct 5–31 comes from per-game tracker peaks with dates (Ride A Pet Oct 3, Steal Animal Egg Oct 10, Anime Dice Oct 18, Break & Steal Oct 18, DONT SPLASH late Oct).
- **Trackers disagree wildly.** Steal An Egg's peak reads 2.2M, 2.6M or 14.3M; Break and Steal reads 30K or 1.3M on different RoWatcher pages; Steal Fish Eggs reads 3K, 16K or 46K. RoWatcher splits listings by title tag ([HEAVEN], [UPD], [EVENT]), which creates duplicate pages with different peaks. Use ranges only.
- **Event spikes.** Ride A Pet's 1.6M and Break & Steal's 1.3M look like single-day or event peaks (Halloween events, admin-abuse events). Sustained 7-day averages are about 90K and about 130K.
- Profitable.app revenue is modeled from visits, not reported.
- RoTrends announced deprecation (Aug 31). Its pages may be frozen, so RoTrends snapshots may be stale even when undated.
- Roblox's official charts (Up-and-Coming / Top Trending) could not be read for October 2026; search only returned the 2025 versions.
- No "50KCCU"-style milestone codes were found for October games. The closest were "1MVISITS" (Steal Animal Egg) and older "100MilVisits" / "THANKYOUFOR100M" codes on Every-Second games (STALE).
- Listicle and SEO sites (cubix, exitlag, Indonesian trend blogs, "best Roblox games 2026") were seen but not used as evidence.
