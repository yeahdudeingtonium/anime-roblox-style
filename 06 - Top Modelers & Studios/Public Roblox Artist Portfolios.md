# Public Roblox Artist Portfolios

## TL;DR
- Roblox anime-game artists mostly publish on **carrd sites, DevForum "Showcase Your Portfolio" threads and X**, not ArtStation. Expect **Studio video clips, credits lists of games and a price/terms section**.
- VFX artists in the genre credit **shipped anime and combat games**. Examples: `Plebarae` (Combat Warriors, Kaizen, Anime Champions X, Mythfall), and a Khan VFX carrd (Jujutsu Infinite, Shonen Unleashed, Heroes Battlegrounds, Project Baki).
- Modellers advertise **Blender + Substance Painter**, separate **stylized vs realistic** sections, and PBR + emission maps (`RosariaDev`, `Ticoelephant`, `Rxgnar`).
- Strong portfolios state **policies**: self-made VFX without packs (`assetstring`), intake forms, 2 revision rounds, licence terms (`Checcerr`: non-exclusive single-experience licence after full payment).
- What studios ask for (for example the Jujutsu Annals hiring post): **video showcases of previous Roblox VFX, preferably anime or combat**, covering combat VFX, ultimates, domain expansions and impacts.
- Use the rubric below to grade portfolios in 5 minutes. Use the same rubric to build your own.

## Why it matters for anime battlegrounds
Most genre teams are a small core plus freelancers. Hiring well, and pricing fairly, depends on reading portfolios quickly and spotting red flags (pack-flipping, render-only work, no credits).

## How to do it

| Venue | What you'll find | Notes |

| BuiltByBit / Creator Store | VFX packs | Useful to spot portfolios that resell packs |

### Step 3: The 5-minute portfolio rubric
Score 0–2 each (10 = hire-ready for a battlegrounds game):

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In-engine proof | Renders only | Some Studio screenshots | Studio video at game camera distance |
| 2 | Genre fit | Unrelated | Generic fantasy | Anime / combat moves with startup → impact → dissipate |
| 3 | Technical honesty | No specs | Some | Tri counts, texture sizes, particle counts, wireframes |
| 4 | Credits | None | Unshipped projects | Shipped games, verifiable |
| 5 | Terms clarity | None | Prices only | Prices + revisions + payment + rights |

Red flags:
- VFX that look identical to marketplace packs (BuiltByBit and the like) with no "self-made" statement.
- Only black-background VFX renders, never in a lit scene.
- Weapons at 20K tris "for quality".
- No rights or licence terms (it'll be argued about later).
- Unrealistic speed claims ("full moveset in 1 day").

### Step 4: What hiring teams ask for
The Jujutsu Annals DevForum posts (a JJK-inspired battlegrounds project) ask for:
- A portfolio or **video showcases of previous Roblox VFX work, preferably anime or combat**.
- Scope: combat VFX, ultimate move effects, **domain expansion effects**, impact effects.

Other anime-game hiring posts on the DevForum (2026) bundle **animator + character designer/modeller + VFX artist + 3D modeller**, a sign that teams want people who can cover several roles. Plan briefs per discipline anyway (see [[Commissioning and Briefing Artists]]).

### Step 5: Build your own portfolio to this standard
1. **Hero reel (30–60 s)**: best 5 moves or assets, in Studio, at gameplay distance, with sound.
2. **Breakdowns**: for VFX, the emitter list and textures used; for models, wireframe + tris + texture sheet.
3. **Credits** with game links and your role.
4. **Prices** as ranges in R$ and USD, revision policy, payment schedule, rights policy.
5. **Contact**: Discord + email; an intake form if you're busy.
6. Host on carrd plus a DevForum showcase thread; post clips on X.

- [ ] Score each with the rubric (≥7/10 to proceed)
- [ ] Verify one credit per artist with in-game proof

- [ ] Paid test task (one move or one prop) before a long engagement

## Common Mistakes
- Hiring off a single flashy render.
- Taking "worked on X" at face value.
- Ignoring licence terms: a non-exclusive single-experience licence isn't ownership.
- Expecting ArtStation-style portfolios; Roblox artists live on carrd and Discord.
- Underpaying top VFX artists, then losing them mid-update.
