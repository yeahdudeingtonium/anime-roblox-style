# Case Study - Studio-Scale and Licensed Anime Games

## TL;DR
- Anime on Roblox runs on **two tracks**: (1) fan-made "inspired-by" games (Blox Fruits, GPO, Type Soul, Project Mugetsu, Sakura Stand, Anime Vanguards) from small-to-mid teams, and (2) **officially licensed** games from professional studios (Gamefam + Crunchyroll's My Hero Academia; Twin Atlas + Toei's *Hypergalactic*).
- **Gamefam** calls itself the first company to officially license a AAA anime for Roblox (*My Hero Academia: Battlegrounds*, Dec 2023, with Crunchyroll; *MHA Ultimate* in 2025). Fan MHA games were reportedly removed around that launch (alleged DMCA; unconfirmed).
- **Twin Atlas** (formed 2022 from the RedManta + Sonar Studios merger, about 100 people, Roblox Studio of the Year 2023) built *Hypergalactic: Monkey Quest* for Toei Animation (Nov 2024). Its art leadership talks about **broad-audience visual style, instance streaming and mesh simplification**.
- **Kitawari / Anime Vanguards** shows the business side: a 2026 federal lawsuit alleges a contributor's revenue share was pressured from 20% to 10% and puts the game's 2025 earnings above **$12 M** (plaintiff's claims).
- **Blox Fruits** (Gamer Robot Inc), **Project Mugetsu** (Osiris Productions), **Sakura Stand** (Sakura Pro Max, reportedly a single main developer) and **Type Soul** have almost **no public art-pipeline information**. Treat any named credits with caution.
- Takeaway: if you want to scale, the pro studios show the playbook: **concept artists + environment artists + streaming-aware tech + written contracts + IP clarity**.

## Why it matters for anime battlegrounds
Licensed studios set the quality ceiling and the legal reality. Fan-made giants show what a small team can reach, and what goes wrong (IP takedowns, revenue disputes) when structure is missing.

### Twin Atlas × Toei Animation (Hypergalactic)
| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Studio | Formed 2022 from the RedManta + Sonar Studios merger; Roblox Studio of the Year 2023; described as a ~100-person studio |
| Game | *Hypergalactic: Monkey Quest* (Nov 2024), the core Roblox hub for Toei's *Hypergalactic* film IP, connecting to other licensed games via Spaceport |
| IP origin | Film characters co-created with Naoto Ohshima (Sonic) |
| Art leadership | Mary Rukavina, Director of Creativity (Creatures of Sonaria creator) |
| Art approach (interviews) | Visual style aimed at "everyone who enjoys games and fantasy", not only kids; instance streaming to load by what players see; meshes changed to hull to cut size |
| People | `Fluorlite`, a known Roblox showcase artist, is now a full-time environment artist at Twin Atlas and builds natively in Studio, using the Creator Store and excited about procedural models |
| Hiring | Listings for 2D concept artists ("characters, creatures, environments, props and items") and a 3D animator for an "anime project" |

What to copy:
- **Dedicated concept art before 3D**, even for Roblox.
- **Streaming and simplification as art-direction tools**: fidelity where players look, cheap geometry elsewhere.
- Native-Studio environment artists working alongside Blender modellers.

## Fan-made giants

### Anime Vanguards (Kitawari)
- Tower-defence anime game by the **Kitawari** group (fan wiki: founded by `Yuruzuu`, owned by `KitawariHolder`).
- **Lawsuit (filed May 22, SDNY)**: Rehaab Haque and Boss Studio LLC v. Vladislav Popov and Andrei Acuña, over ownership, revenue and copyrighted work used in the game. Alleged: the game recovered to 100K+ CCU with the plaintiff's help, his share was pressured from 20% to 10% in Dec 2025, revenue was over $12 M in 2025 and at least $2.7 M through May 2026, and projected revenue is $52 M+ for 2026–2030. **All plaintiff allegations.**
- Lesson: **written agreements on revenue share and IP for every contributor**, including artists. See [[Commissioning and Briefing Artists]].

### Blox Fruits (Gamer Robot Inc)
- One Piece-inspired RPG; Gamer Robot Inc is consistently named as the studio.
- Low-quality sources name `mygame43` (balance and code) and `Rip_Indra` (visual direction, world-building).
- Revenue estimates vary wildly ($8–12 M/month to $100 M/year), none authoritative.
- A Change.org petition asks Toei to act against it; no takedown was found.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The `mygame43` / `Rip_Indra` role split comes from low-quality and possibly AI-generated pages. Don't publish it as fact.

### Project Mugetsu (Osiris Productions)
- Bleach-inspired; guides credit **Osiris Productions** as developer and as the source of the game's artwork. No individual art credits were found.

### Sakura Stand
- JoJo-inspired with JJK and Dragon Ball influences; a guide says it's a revamp by **Sakura Pro Max**, and another says there's **one main developer**. Updates go through Trello.

### Type Soul
- Bleach-inspired. A January 2025 blog lists six names on the team (`K1LLUAA`, `vvithdraw`, `Khaotxc`, `4Data`, `drakoooos`, `neighborhood361`) without roles. **Unverified; don't attribute art to any of them.** Official channels are the game's Discord and Trello.

### Grand Piece Online
- No usable public source on the team or pipeline turned up. Searches returned unrelated "Grand Quest" projects.

## Adjacent: anime professionals entering Roblox
- *The Inn* (Studio Mayflower): a Japanese horror roguelite directed by **Ryu Nakayama**, director of the 2022 *Chainsaw Man* TV anime and making his first game. He said he is "deliberately trying to give it a stronger Japanese feel". There's a Roblox Creator Spotlight on the DevForum. A sign that anime-industry talent is treating Roblox as a real platform.

## Checklist
- [ ] Decide: original IP, fan-inspired, or pursue a licence (Gamefam's creator programme, Spaceport-style deals)
- [ ] If scaling: hire concept art before 3D
- [ ] Written agreements with every contributor (art included)
- [ ] Art direction built around streaming and simplification
- [ ] Don't cite unverified team rosters publicly

## Common Mistakes
- Assuming fan-anime success is safe from IP action.
- Treating revenue-share handshakes as contracts.
- Attributing art to named developers from low-quality roster pages.
- Ignoring the licensed studios' quality bar when pitching to IP holders.
